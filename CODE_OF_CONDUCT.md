# Code of Conduct

This is a learning resource. People contributing here are, by definition, often still learning —
including the beginner fixing their first-ever typo PR.

- Be patient with questions that seem basic. Everyone in this course's own target audience was
  once the person who didn't know what a staging area was.
- Critique the content, not the person. "This command is wrong" is useful; anything aimed at the
  contributor instead of the contribution isn't.
- No harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks, in issues, PRs, or comments — no exceptions.

Violations can be reported by opening an issue or contacting the maintainer directly through
GitHub. Reports are taken seriously and handled privately.
